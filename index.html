// About me

Curious. Analytic. Adventurous.

Welcome to my homebuilt website. I am a Swede in my mid-twenties with a large appetite for sport and for places I have not seen yet — preferably both at once. I live in Skellefteå, Sweden, where I spend my working days analysing electricity markets. The rest of the time goes to training, to learning something new — a language, a dish, or how to build a site like this one — and to working out where to ride next.

I ride because a bicycle moves at exactly the right speed: fast enough to cross a country, slow enough to notice it. There are no sponsors behind any of this and no predetermined goal — the journeys run on curiosity alone… and on gelato. If you want the long version, each trip has its own page, and the journal has it day by day.
