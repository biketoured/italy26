Riding for research
that saves lives

Each day people around us are affected by different kinds of brain diseases. While some of them are rather harmless, other are fatal or incurable. They all have real impact on people we care about.

I ride this journey partly in memory of my father and everyone else fighting brain cancer. When he was diagnosed in 2017, everything changed. See more…

100% of every donation goes directly to Hjärnfonden. No middlemen and no charges. I covered every cost of the trip myself. The ride finished in June 2026, but the collection is still open, and every krona given from here on still goes straight to the fund.
